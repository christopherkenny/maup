# maup: Sampling Solutions to the Modifiable Areal Unit Problem

Downloads simulated Census tract maps and plans for every U.S. county
across the 2000, 2010, and 2020 decennial Censuses from the Harvard
Dataverse. This data supports the study of the Modifiable Areal Unit
Problem by allowing researchers to quantify how sensitive any
tract-level statistic is to the choice of geographic boundaries. Builds
on data from Brown, Kenny, and Simko (2025)
[doi:10.31235/osf.io/nv5up_v2](https://doi.org/10.31235/osf.io/nv5up_v2)
.
